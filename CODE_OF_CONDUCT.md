# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in GuardianO3's public repositories (issues, pull requests,
reviews and discussions) is expected to follow the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep personal
attacks, harassment and discriminatory language out of every space we run.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **support@guardiano3.com**. Reports are read
by the maintainers and handled in confidence. We may edit or remove comments,
close threads, or block accounts that break this code.
